01 / TLM

iRobot Home

Senior Android Developer

2023 - 2026

A consumer robotics app for managing robotic vacuum cleaners, maps, and cleaning tasks.

My contribution

  • Developed live maps, 2D/3D map interactions, map editing, and real-time robot status UI.
  • Owned key screens, including the main experience for multiple robots and maps and the Robot Details screen.
  • Built reusable Jetpack Compose components and contributed to performance, reliability, testing, and release support.
  • Designed and implemented two- and three-finger 3D map controls for A/B testing. Results favored the two-finger scheme, with more active use of rotation and tilt; three-finger controls saw limited use even with onboarding.

Technologies: Kotlin, Jetpack Compose, Coroutines/Flow, Hilt, 2D/3D rendering
